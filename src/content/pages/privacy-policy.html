Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information {{ client.legalName }}, doing business as {{ client.name }} ("we," "us," or "our"), collects through this website and how we use it. We keep it simple: we collect only what we need to respond to you, and we don't sell your information.

Information You Give Us

When you fill out our contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide. That typically includes:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number and email address
  • Your address or general location, if you include it
  • A description of your plumbing issue or project

If you call or email us directly, we receive whatever information you share in that conversation.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, this site is served by a hosting provider that keeps basic server logs. These logs may include your IP address, browser type, the pages you requested, and the date and time of your visit. They are used to keep the site running and secure. We do not use this website to run advertising trackers. Our contact form uses a spam check that may process basic technical information about your visit to confirm you're a person.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you send us to:

  • Respond to your questions and requests
  • Schedule and provide plumbing services you ask for
  • Follow up about work we've done or discussed with you
  • Keep records needed to run our business

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

How Form Submissions Are Handled

Our contact form is processed by Netlify, the service that hosts this website. When you submit the form, your information is transmitted to and stored by Netlify so it can be delivered to us. Netlify handles that data under its own privacy policy. Beyond our hosting and form provider, we share your information only when needed to provide a service you've requested or when required by law.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your information only as long as we need it to respond to you, provide services, and meet our business and legal obligations.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No website or internet transmission is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive details, like payment card numbers, through the contact form.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update or delete the personal information you've sent us, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Just reach out using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes take effect when they are posted on this page. This policy is governed by the laws of the state in which {{ client.name }} operates.
