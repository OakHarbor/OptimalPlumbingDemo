  • Watch for a water bill that rises without explanation, the sound of running water when everything is off, musty smells, stains or soft spots on walls and ceilings, warm spots on floors, or a water meter that keeps moving with all fixtures off. Any of these is worth a call. Hidden leaks do more damage the longer they run.

  • Shut off the water. For a single fixture, use the shutoff valve under the sink or behind the toilet. For a burst pipe or major leak, close your main water shutoff valve. For a leaking water heater, close its cold supply valve and turn off the gas or breaker. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside.
